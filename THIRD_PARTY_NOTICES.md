## ExerciseGymGifsDB

The app can load exercise demonstration GIFs and WebP thumbnails on demand from the tagged `v1.1.0` release of [ExerciseGymGifsDB](https://github.com/JahelCuadrado/ExerciseGymGifsDB), served through jsDelivr. The media files are not copied into this repository or bundled in the app.

The upstream repository states that its author collected the images from the Internet, does not own their copyright, and cannot grant rights to third parties. The GIFs belong to their respective authors. This integration is intended for private evaluation. Replace these URLs with media you own or have licensed before distributing the app.

The source can be disabled or replaced per exercise by saving a custom HTTPS demonstration link in the app.

## 3D muscle navigator

The current interactive human figure is generated at runtime from SceneKit geometry written for this project. It does not include or derive geometry from Z-Anatomy. It is a training-region navigator, not a medical anatomy model or diagnostic aid.
